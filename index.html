About

I am a graduate student researcher in the Saul Lab at Miami University's Department of Chemical, Paper, and Biomedical Engineering. My current work asks why artificial ovary implants, a cell-based hormone therapy, show declining estrogen and progesterone secretion over time.

Before Miami, I worked on bacterial invasion of the blood–brain barrier at Old Dominion University, on tumor-associated macrophages in chronic myelogenous leukemia at AIIMS New Delhi, and on long non-coding RNAs in breast cancer at Banaras Hindu University, where I earned an M.Sc. in Biochemistry.

Research

Graduate Student Researcher, Miami University

Aug 2025 – present

Saul Lab, Department of Chemical, Paper, and Biomedical Engineering

  • Investigated why cell-based hormone therapy via artificial ovary implantation loses function over time, resulting in declining estrogen and progesterone secretion.
  • Developed a mechanistic model of the hypothalamic–pituitary–ovarian axis with time-dependent ovarian capacity and enzyme-kinetic hormone production, capturing the transition from initial endocrine recovery to construct decline and compensatory increases in LH and FSH.
  • Testing model predictions through biological experiments in ovariectomized rat models to identify the mechanisms underlying long-term construct failure.

Graduate Research Assistant, Old Dominion University

Jan 2024 – May 2025

Cellular and Molecular Microbiology Laboratory, Norfolk, VA

Signaling pathways after infection with Listeria monocytogenes

  • Utilized single-cell proteomics to identify upregulated and downregulated pathways in mouse brain following infection.
  • Established an in vivo Listeria monocytogenes infection model in mice via tail vein injection to study brain invasion.
  • Isolated brain endothelial cells for proteomic analysis of infection-induced pathway changes, and processed brain tissue for histological staining to localize bacterial presence within the CNS.

Factors responsible for invasion of the blood–brain barrier

  • Performed ligand overlay assays and co-immunoprecipitation using proteins extracted from human brain cells, followed by Western blot analysis.
  • Conducted LC-MS/MS analysis of gel bands with protein identification using FASTA database searches and BLAST sequence alignment.

Transendothelial migration of infected monocytes

  • Developed and validated an in vitro blood–brain barrier model using brain endothelial cells, astrocytes, and pericytes, with TEER measurements.
  • Established a Trojan horse model by infecting human monocytes with L. monocytogenes, and studied migration by assessing barrier disruption and quantifying migrated infected monocytes.
